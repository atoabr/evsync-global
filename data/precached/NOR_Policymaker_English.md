# Executive Briefing for Norway's Policymaker on EV Transition

## Headline
Norway's electric vehicle (EV) transition is nearly complete, with a 97.8% EV adoption rate for new car sales in 2026. Strategic actions are needed to maintain leadership and address supply chain vulnerabilities.

## Situation
Norway has achieved its target of 100% zero-emission new car sales by 2025, with 97.8% of new cars sold in 2026 being electric [Gridserve](https://www.gridserve.com/why-norway-leads-the-world-in-ev-adoption/). The country benefits from a robust renewable energy infrastructure, with 99.1% of electricity generated from renewable sources as of 2021 [World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS?locations=NO). However, the reduction in VAT exemptions and the introduction of weight-based taxes on EVs could impact future adoption rates [Alternative Fuels Observatory](https://alternative-fuels-observatory.ec.europa.eu/transport-mode/road/norway/incentives-legislations).

## Prioritized Recommendations

1. **Enhance Battery Recycling Infrastructure**
   - **Impact**: High. Reduces dependency on raw material imports and mitigates environmental impact.
   - **Cost**: Medium (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: 2-3 years.
   - **Rationale**: Norway's battery recycling market is projected to grow significantly [Markets and Markets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/geography/lithium-ion-battery-recycling-market/norway).

2. **Promote Second-Life Battery Applications**
   - **Impact**: High. Utilizes existing resources for energy storage, enhancing grid stability.
   - **Cost**: Low (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: 1-2 years.
   - **Rationale**: Leveraging second-life batteries can provide cost-effective energy storage solutions [Green Stocks Research](https://greenstocksresearch.com/battery-metals-stocks/).

3. **Diversify Critical Mineral Supply Chains**
   - **Impact**: Medium. Reduces risk from supply chain disruptions.
   - **Cost**: Medium (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: 3-5 years.
   - **Rationale**: High concentration of refining in China poses a risk [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-inventory-of-export-restrictions-on-critical-raw-materials-2026_d5ca8f62-en/full-report.html).

4. **Expand Charging Infrastructure in Rural Areas**
   - **Impact**: Medium. Supports equitable access to EVs across the country.
   - **Cost**: High (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: 2-4 years.
   - **Rationale**: Current infrastructure is concentrated on major roads [Alternative Fuels Observatory](https://alternative-fuels-observatory.ec.europa.eu/transport-mode/road/norway/incentives-legislations).

5. **Monitor and Adjust EV Tax Policies**
   - **Impact**: Medium. Ensures continued EV adoption and market stability.
   - **Cost**: Low (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: Ongoing.
   - **Rationale**: Recent tax changes could impact EV sales [IEA](https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/data-tools/global-ev-policy-explorer).

## Oil-Displacement and CO2 Impact
- **MODEL ESTIMATE**: With 10% fleet electrification, Norway could displace 6,617 barrels of oil per day, avoiding 864,000 tonnes of CO2 annually. Assumptions: 10% of car fleet, 15,000 km/year, 8.0 L/100km, 16 kWh/100km, oil $80/bbl, oil import share 0%, grid 30 gCO2/kWh.

## Supply Chain Risks
- **High Concentration Risks**: Cobalt and nickel mining are highly concentrated in DR Congo and Indonesia, respectively, with significant refining in China [Mineral Concentration Data] [unverified].
- **Export Restrictions**: Potential restrictions on critical materials could impact supply chains [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-inventory-of-export-restrictions-on-critical-raw-materials-2026_d5ca8f62-en/full-report.html).

## 12-Month Watchlist
1. **Monitor Battery Recycling Market Growth**: Track developments in recycling technologies and market expansion.
2. **Evaluate Impact of EV Tax Changes**: Assess how recent tax adjustments affect EV sales and market dynamics.
3. **Track Global Mineral Supply Chain Developments**: Stay informed on geopolitical changes affecting mineral supplies.
4. **Assess Charging Infrastructure Expansion**: Ensure rural areas are adequately served by charging networks.
5. **Review Renewable Energy Export Policies**: Monitor potential changes in electricity export regulations due to resource constraints [Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/norway-to-impose-power-export-controls/) [weak source].

This briefing provides a strategic overview to guide Norway's continued leadership in the EV transition while addressing emerging challenges and opportunities.
