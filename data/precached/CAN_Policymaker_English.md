# Executive Briefing for Canadian Policymaker

## Headline
Accelerating Canada's Transition to Electric Vehicles: Strategic Recommendations for a Sustainable and Energy-Secure Future

## Situation
As of 2026, Canada is actively pursuing a transition to electric vehicles (EVs) with a 25% EV sales share of new cars [IEA Global EV Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/trends-in-electric-cars). The country has scrapped its national EV sales mandate for 2026 but maintains a target of 100% zero-emission vehicles by 2035 [CBT News](https://www.cbtnews.com/canada-drops-ev-mandate-2026/). Current policies include purchase incentives and a federal subsidy program [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/canada-rolls-back-ev-regulations-boosts-incentives-2026-02-05/). However, challenges such as high tariffs on Chinese-made EVs and reliance on foreign mineral refining persist [Motor Illustrated](https://motorillustrated.com/canada-might-revive-ev-rebates-reassess-tariffs-on-chinese-cars/167301/).

## Prioritized Recommendations

1. **Enhance Domestic Battery Refining and Manufacturing**
   - **Impact**: High. Reduces reliance on foreign refining, strengthens energy security.
   - **Cost**: High (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: Medium-term (3-5 years).
   - **Rationale**: With China dominating refining (e.g., 65% of lithium refining), investing in domestic capabilities can mitigate supply chain risks [Seed Data].

2. **Expand Public Charging Infrastructure**
   - **Impact**: High. Supports increased EV adoption by addressing range anxiety.
   - **Cost**: Medium (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: Short-term (1-2 years).
   - **Rationale**: With 5,400 new stations planned, further expansion is crucial to meet growing demand [Natural Resources Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/natural-resources-canada/news/2026/02/canada-advances-new-auto-strategy-by-expanding-ev-charging-infrastructure-across-the-country.html).

3. **Implement Battery Recycling Programs**
   - **Impact**: Medium. Reduces environmental impact and resource dependency.
   - **Cost**: Medium (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: Medium-term (3-5 years).
   - **Rationale**: Recycling initiatives can position Canada as a leader in sustainable practices [Critical MB](https://www.criticalmb.com/p/new-black-mass-export-rules-will).

4. **Reassess Import Tariffs on EVs**
   - **Impact**: Medium. Encourages market competition and lowers consumer costs.
   - **Cost**: Low (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: Short-term (1-2 years).
   - **Rationale**: Revising the 100% tariff on Chinese EVs could enhance affordability and choice [Motor Illustrated](https://motorillustrated.com/canada-might-revive-ev-rebates-reassess-tariffs-on-chinese-cars/167301/).

5. **Promote Second-Life Battery Applications**
   - **Impact**: Medium. Enhances grid stability and energy storage solutions.
   - **Cost**: Medium (judgment).
   - **Timeline**: Medium-term (3-5 years).
   - **Rationale**: Repurposing EV batteries for energy storage can provide economic and environmental benefits [Electra Battery Materials](https://electrabmc.com/news/first-cobalt-unveils-strategic-shift-to-make-battery-precursor-and-nickel-sulfate-changes-name-to-electra-battery-materials).

## Oil-Displacement and CO2 Impact
**MODEL ESTIMATE**: At a 30% EV share, Canada could displace 161,282 barrels of oil per day, avoiding 19,375,200 tonnes of CO2 annually. This assumes 7,800,000 EVs, 15,000 km/year, and a grid emission factor of 120 gCO2/kWh.

## Supply Chain Risks
Canada faces high concentration risks in critical mineral refining, with China controlling 65% of lithium and 76% of cobalt refining [Seed Data]. Export restrictions and geopolitical tensions further threaten supply chain stability [STR Trade Report](https://www.strtrade.com/trade-news-resources/str-trade-report/trade-report/august/export-restrictions-imposed-on-some-critical-minerals).

## 12-Month Watchlist
1. **Review of Interim ZEV Targets**: Monitor federal reviews for potential changes in interim targets.
2. **Tariff Policy Changes**: Track developments in import tariff policies on Chinese EVs.
3. **Public Charging Infrastructure Expansion**: Assess progress in the deployment of new charging stations.
4. **Battery Recycling Initiatives**: Observe advancements in recycling technologies and policies.
5. **Global Mineral Supply Chain Dynamics**: Stay informed on geopolitical developments affecting mineral supply chains.

This briefing outlines strategic actions to enhance Canada's EV transition, focusing on domestic capabilities, infrastructure expansion, and policy adjustments to ensure a sustainable and energy-secure future.
