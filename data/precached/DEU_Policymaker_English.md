# Executive Briefing for German Policymaker on EV Transition

## Headline
Accelerating Germany's transition to electric vehicles (EVs) is crucial for reducing oil dependency, enhancing energy security, and achieving climate goals. Strategic actions can optimize economic and environmental outcomes.

## Situation
Germany's EV market is rapidly growing, with a 29.3% sales share in 2026 [source](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/europe-ev-market-share-tops-25-july-france-germany-drive-growth-2026-08-23/). Current policies include purchase subsidies, tax exemptions, and significant investments in charging infrastructure [source](https://alternative-fuels-observatory.ec.europa.eu/general-information/news/germanys-2026-ev-incentive-programme-supporting-socially-targeted-ev). However, the country remains heavily reliant on energy imports (70.49% in 2023) [source](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/DEU/indicator/EG.IMP.CONS.ZS), and faces supply chain risks due to high concentration in critical minerals like cobalt and graphite.

## Prioritized Recommendations

1. **Expand Charging Infrastructure**
   - **Impact**: High. Facilitates EV adoption by reducing range anxiety.
   - **Cost**: Medium (judgment). Supported by €900 million investment [weak source](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1235431473831592/posts/1857542651620468/).
   - **Timeline**: Short-term (1-2 years). Accelerate deployment of fast-charging stations to complement existing 196,000 public points [source](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/electric-vehicle-charging-chap-6-and-10).

2. **Enhance Battery Recycling Programs**
   - **Impact**: High. Reduces dependency on imported raw materials and mitigates environmental impact.
   - **Cost**: Medium (judgment). Leverage existing recycling technologies [source](https://www.greenli-ion.com/post/bis-black-mass-export-restrictions-directive-allocation-order).
   - **Timeline**: Medium-term (2-3 years). Develop infrastructure for large-scale recycling operations.

3. **Promote Domestic Battery Production**
   - **Impact**: High. Strengthens supply chain resilience and creates jobs.
   - **Cost**: High (judgment). Requires significant investment in technology and facilities.
   - **Timeline**: Medium-term (3-5 years). Focus on leveraging potential lithium reserves [source](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/electric-vehicle-batteries).

4. **Increase Renewable Energy Integration**
   - **Impact**: High. Reduces CO2 emissions from EV charging and enhances energy security.
   - **Cost**: Medium (judgment). Aligns with existing renewable energy targets.
   - **Timeline**: Medium-term (3-5 years). Accelerate the transition to renewable sources, currently at 39.83% [source](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/DEU/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS).

5. **Implement Consumer Education Campaigns**
   - **Impact**: Medium. Increases public awareness and acceptance of EVs.
   - **Cost**: Low (judgment). Utilize digital platforms and public events.
   - **Timeline**: Short-term (1 year). Focus on benefits of EVs and available incentives.

## Oil-Displacement and CO2 Impact
- **MODEL ESTIMATE**: At 30% electrification of the car fleet, Germany could displace 303,955 barrels of oil per day, avoid an import bill of approximately $8.7 billion annually, and reduce CO2 emissions by 28.4 million tonnes per year. Assumptions include 15,000 km/year per vehicle, oil at $80/bbl, and a grid emission factor of 350 gCO2/kWh.

## Supply Chain Risks
Germany faces significant supply chain risks due to high concentration in critical minerals:
- **Cobalt**: 74% mined in DR Congo, 76% refined in China [unverified].
- **Graphite**: 77% mined and 90% refined in China [unverified].
- **Battery Cells**: 75% manufactured in China [unverified].

## 12-Month Watchlist
1. **Monitor EV Sales Trends**: Track progress towards the 52% sales share target by 2030.
2. **Evaluate Charging Infrastructure Expansion**: Assess the effectiveness of new installations.
3. **Track Battery Recycling Developments**: Follow advancements in recycling technologies and capacity.
4. **Observe Renewable Energy Integration**: Monitor increases in renewable energy share.
5. **Supply Chain Stability**: Watch for geopolitical developments affecting mineral supply chains, especially regarding China and DR Congo.

This briefing outlines strategic actions to enhance Germany's EV transition, focusing on infrastructure, supply chain resilience, and renewable integration to achieve energy security and environmental goals.
